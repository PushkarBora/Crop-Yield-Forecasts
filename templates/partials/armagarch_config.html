ℹ️ ARMA-GARCH vs AR-GARCH vs AR-EGARCH

The MA component captures moving average dynamics in the mean equation, which AR-GARCH lacks. Univariate → ARMA(ar,ma)-GARCH(p,q)  |  Multivariate → ARMAX(ar,ma)-GARCH(p,q). No leverage/asymmetry term — use AR-EGARCH if asymmetric volatility response is expected.
