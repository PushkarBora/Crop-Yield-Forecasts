ℹ️ AR-GARCH vs AR-EGARCH

AR-GARCH has no leverage/asymmetry term — positive and negative shocks affect volatility equally. Univariate → AR(k)-GARCH(p,q)  |  Multivariate → ARX(k)-GARCH(p,q). Use AR-EGARCH if you expect asymmetric volatility responses.
