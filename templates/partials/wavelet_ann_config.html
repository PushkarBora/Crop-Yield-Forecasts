🎛️ Manual Wavelet-ANN Configuration

💡 How it works: Target series is decomposed via MODWT into detail (W1…WJ) and smooth (VJ) coefficients. A separate ANN is trained on each coefficient. Forecasts are summed to produce the final prediction. MC Dropout (300 samples) generates 50%, 80% and 95% uncertainty intervals.
