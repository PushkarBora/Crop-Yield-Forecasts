🎛️ Manual Wavelet-Transformer Configuration

💡 How it works: Target series is decomposed via circular MODWT into detail (W₁…Wⱼ) and smooth (Vⱼ) coefficients. A separate encoder-only Transformer (CoeffTransformer) is trained on each coefficient. Forecasts are summed. MC Dropout (300 samples) generates 50%, 80% and 95% uncertainty intervals. dim_feedforward = max(d_model × 2, 16) is auto-computed.
