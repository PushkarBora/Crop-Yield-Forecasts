Data Collection

DATAI follows a strict privacy-first approach:

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information
  • We do not use third-party advertising tools
  • We do not share your data with any third parties
  • We collect only anonymized usage data (e.g., feature usage frequency) to improve platform performance and user experience
✓ What This Means for You
Your use of DATAI is secure, and we ensure that no unauthorized access occurs to any part of your interaction with the platform. Your privacy is guaranteed at every step.
🗂️

Data Policy

DATAI operates on a temporary, cloud-based computing environment:

💡 How It Works

No uploaded data is stored permanently.

Every analysis session is a virtual instance. Once the session ends (i.e., when the user closes the app or navigates away), all uploaded files, temporary outputs, and analysis results are permanently deleted.

  • No backup copies are created
  • No user input is saved unless explicitly exported by the user
  • No file retention - everything is deleted after your session
  • No metadata storage beyond the duration of your session
🔒 Your Data Security
This ensures that your data remains confidential and fully under your control. We do not retain any files, results, or metadata beyond the duration of your session.
⏱️

Session Management

Understanding how DATAI handles your analysis sessions:

⚠️ Important to Know

Once you quit, everything in that session is lost forever.

No uploaded data is stored anywhere. When your session ends, all data is permanently erased from our servers with no possibility of recovery.

Session Lifecycle:

  • Upload: Your data is temporarily loaded into memory
  • Analysis: Models process your data in real-time
  • Results: You can view and export your results
  • Session End: All data is immediately and permanently deleted
👤

Your Rights

As a DATAI user, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Privacy: Your data is never accessed by anyone except you
  • Right to Control: You decide what to upload and when to delete it
  • Right to Export: Download your results before ending your session
  • Right to Delete: Simply close your session to permanently erase all data
  • Right to Know: Full transparency about how your data is handled
🛡️

Security Measures

DATAI implements industry-standard security practices:

  • Encrypted transmission of all data between your device and our servers
  • Isolated sessions ensuring no data leakage between users
  • Automatic deletion of all temporary files after session ends
  • Secure computing environment with restricted access
  • Regular security audits to maintain platform integrity
🌾

About DATAI

DATAI (Data Analysis Through AI models) is a research project developed under ICAR-IASRI (Indian Council of Agricultural Research - Indian Agricultural Statistics Research Institute), New Delhi, India.

Our mission is to provide cutting-edge AI-powered tools for agricultural data analysis while maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and security.
